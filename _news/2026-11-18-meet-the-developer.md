I'm **Abdul Mueed**, a full-stack developer from Pakistan. I study Business & Information Technology at Virtual University of Pakistan, and outside of coursework I build and ship software in the open — mostly on [GitHub](https://github.com/am-abdulmueed), mostly in Kotlin and JavaScript.

This post is a quick map of everything I've got running right now, why each thing exists, and where you can use it.

## Why I build in the open

Most of what I learn comes from reading other people's code. Building in public is my way of returning the favour — every project here is source-available, and the ones with real users stay free.

I also like projects that finish something. A snippet tool you can actually send to a friend. A streaming app that just plays the video. Small, sharp tools beat big, vague ideas.

## What I'm building

### PluginStream — an Android streaming app that doesn't sell you out

[PluginStream](https://github.com/am-abdulmueed/pluginstream) is my biggest project. It brings movies, TV series and anime into one clean interface on Android.

- A **plugin and extension architecture**, so new sources can be added without rewriting the app
- **Built-in ad blocking** — no account, no sign-up, no tracking
- Quality options from **360p up to 4K**, plus **subtitles and Chromecast**
- Ships as a **direct APK** from GitHub releases, with true auto-update via Shizuku, Sui or Dhizuku

It's written in Kotlin, it's open source, and it's the project I spend the most time on. [Grab the latest APK](https://am-abdulmueed.github.io/download/) or read the [docs site](https://pluginstream.pages.dev/).
